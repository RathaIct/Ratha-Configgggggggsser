Privacy Policy for TT Downloader

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how TT Downloader ("Application", "We", "Us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices.

2. Data We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not require account registration, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII) to use this Application. We do not host, store, or monitor your downloaded media files or pasted video URLs on external servers. All download operations are processed locally on your device.

3. Data Collected via Third-Party Services

To support free access to our features and ensure smooth application functionality, third-party service providers integrated into the Application may collect anonymous device information automatically:

  • Advertising Data (Google AdMob): The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and offer optional rewarded credits. AdMob may collect and process non-sensitive device identifiers, such as your Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and standard Usage Data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and detect fraud.
  • App Analytics & Performance: Anonymous diagnostics and performance metrics may be collected automatically by core device operating systems to improve app stability and crash reporting.

4. Device Permissions Requested

To fulfill its utility functions, the Application may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Storage / Photos & Videos Access: Used exclusively to save requested video files directly to your device's local gallery or storage folder.
  • Network / Internet Access: Required to process user-pasted URLs, fetch media metadata, and display third-party advertisements.

5. How Data is Used

Any anonymous data processed via third-party SDKs is used strictly for:

  • Delivering and maintaining core Application utilities.
  • Serving third-party advertisements and processing opt-in rewarded credit requests.
  • Ensuring network security, fraud prevention, and compliance with distributor policies.

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, we hold no user records to delete or modify. You can control or reset your Advertising ID directly at any time through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID).
